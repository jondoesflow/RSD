When someone you love has ADHD, a small comment or a late text can sometimes land like a punch. That's Rejection Sensitivity Dysphoria (RSD): sudden, intense emotional pain triggered by real or perceived rejection or criticism.

Steady is an iPhone app for partners. It helps you understand what's happening, know what to say in the moment, and find your way back to each other afterwards.

Right now: step-by-step help for a wobble, a storm and the moments after.
Feelings finder: turn body sensations and thoughts into words you can share.
Translate: everyday moments, and how to say things so they're heard.
Us: your own plan, with triggers, early signs, what helps and a pause word.
Listen & read: podcasts, books and articles about RSD.

No account. No tracking. Nothing leaves your phone.

Get help with Steady · Privacy policy

Steady is for understanding and connection. It isn't medical advice. If you ever feel unsafe, call your local emergency number, or in the UK call Samaritans free on 116 123.
