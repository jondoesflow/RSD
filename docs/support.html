Common questions

How do I change the names in the app?

Go to the Us tab, tap Edit, and change the names at the top. They update everywhere.

How do I set our pause word?

Us → Edit → Pause word. Pick something neutral or silly you'd never say by accident, and agree a time-out length. Twenty minutes is a good start.

How do I edit our plan?

Us → Edit. Add items with the + button, swipe left to delete, and tap Edit (top left) to reorder. Fill it in together on a calm day.

How do we share moments between our phones?

Open Us → Sync with… on both phones and keep them close together, with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on. No internet is needed. The first time, tap the other phone's name, then tap Accept on the other phone. After that the phones recognise each other and sync whenever both have the Sync screen open. Moments, deletions and plan changes from either phone are merged, and the newest edit wins.

Sync can't find the other phone

Check that both phones have Bluetooth and Wi-Fi switched on, both have the Sync screen open, and that Steady is allowed to use the local network in Settings → Steady → Local Network. Both phones need the same version of Steady.

Where is my data stored? Can I delete it?

Only on your iPhone. Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. See the privacy policy.

Is Steady medical advice?

No. Steady supports understanding between partners. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. If RSD is having a big impact, please talk to a GP or ADHD specialist.

If things feel unsafe

In an emergency, call 999 (UK) or your local emergency number.
Samaritans: call 116 123, free, 24/7.
Shout: text SHOUT to 85258.
NHS 111: choose the mental health option (England).
Steady is for understanding and connection. It isn't medical advice. If you ever feel unsafe, call your local emergency number, or in the UK call Samaritans free on 116 123.
