Last updated: 24 October 2026

Steady is made by Jon Russell, an independent developer. This policy explains how the Steady iPhone app handles your information. In short: Steady does not collect any personal data.

What Steady stores, and where

Everything you enter stays on your own iPhone. That includes your names, your plan (triggers, signs, what helps), your pause word, your note and your moments log. It's saved in the app's private storage on the device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Syncing with your partner's phone. If you use Sync in the Us tab, Steady sends your moments log and plan directly to the paired phone nearby, over Bluetooth or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. The connection is encrypted and goes straight from one phone to the other. It doesn't go through the internet, any server, or the developer. iOS asks for permission to use the local network the first time, and you can turn that off at any time in Settings → Steady.

Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), according to your own backup settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What Steady does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No servers: syncing is phone to phone only.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

Sharing and links

When you choose to send a message (for example a check-in, or a Feelings finder summary), Steady opens the standard iOS share sheet. The message is then sent by the app you pick, such as Messages or WhatsApp, under that app's own privacy policy. Steady never sees or stores what happens after that.

The Listen & read tab links to external websites, podcasts and phone or text support lines. These open outside Steady and are covered by their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Delete individual items inside the app. To remove everything, delete Steady from your iPhone: all of its data is removed with it.

Children

Steady is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
